Stormwatch Castle




**Prompt A – Watercolor Painting**

Create a watercolor painting of a gothic castle on top of a rocky cliff during a stormy sunset. I want the castle to be the main thing people notice when they look at the painting. The sky should have dark storm clouds, rain falling, and big ocean waves crashing against the rocks below. Use watercolour techniques like blending wet colours, soft colour changes, and light layers of paint to make the scene look natural. I want to include different lines, textures, contrast, and movement to make the painting more interesting. Use warm colours like orange, yellow, and red for the sunset, mixed with cool colours like blue, gray, and purple for the storm. The painting should show light and shadows to create depth and a dramatic feeling. My goal is to show how beautiful the castle looks while also showing how powerful and intense the storm can be.
