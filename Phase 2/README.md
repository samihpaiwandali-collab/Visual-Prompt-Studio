Stormwatch Castle




Prompt A – Watercolour Painting

I asked Copilot to create a watercolour painting of a gothic castle atop a rocky cliff during a stormy sunset. I want the castle to be the main focus when people look at the painting. The sky should have dark storm clouds, rain falling, and big ocean waves crashing against the rocks below. Use watercolour techniques like wet-on-wet blending, soft colour changes, and light layers of paint to make the scene look natural. I want to include different lines, textures, contrast, and movement to make the painting more interesting. Use warm colours like orange, yellow, and red for the sunset, mixed with cool colours like blue, gray, and purple for the storm. The painting should show light and shadows to create depth and a dramatic feeling. My goal is to show how beautiful the castle looks while also showing how powerful and intense the storm can be.



Prompt B – Printmaking Linocut


Copilot create a linocut print of a gothic castle on a rocky cliff during a stormy sunset. Emphasize the elements and principles of line, texture, contrast, movement, and emphasis. Use carving tools, a linoleum block, printing ink, and printmaking paper. Apply bold carved lines, high-contrast shapes, and repetitive cut marks characteristic of linocut printmaking. Make the castle the main focal point, with dramatic storm clouds and crashing waves creating movement throughout the image. The linocut process should highlight strong black-and-white contrast, expressive textures, and bold graphic shapes while maintaining the same visual design as the watercolour version.
