Assignment Title
The Sci‑Fi Film Poster Challenge
The Title: Echoes Beyond the Nebula

For my sci‑fi poster, I imagine a massive research outpost built into the side of a glowing nebula. The setting is a quiet observation deck overlooking swirling cosmic clouds, with a single astronaut standing at the edge of a transparent walkway. Sharp geometric lines form the walkway and the station's structural beams, pulling the viewer’s eye straight toward the astronaut. In contrast, soft organic contour lines swirl through the nebula behind them, creating a sense of drifting movement in the background.
The poster's dominant shapes are long rectangular panels that make up the station’s architecture, paired with a large circular viewing dome that frames the nebula. I use positive space to emphasize the astronaut’s silhouette, while the negative space of the vast cosmic background makes the scene feel lonely and expansive.
The poster's textures shift between sleek metallic surfaces on the station and the cloudy, almost powdery texture of the nebula. The station feels solid and heavy, with thick volumes and reinforced beams, while the nebula has no real mass — it feels airy and infinite. To create a strong focal point, I use intense contrast: a bright white light shines directly on the astronaut, while the nebula behind them fades into deep purples and blues. This contrast makes the astronaut the emotional center of the composition.

Vocabulary Breakdown

Lines  
Lines are the paths the eye follows across a composition. They can be straight, curved, sharp, or loose, and they help guide attention or create movement.

Shape  
Shape is the outline or form of an object. It can be geometric or organic, and it helps define structure and visual hierarchy in a design.

Texture  
Texture describes how a surface feels or looks like it would feel. It can be smooth, rough, soft, metallic, or anything that suggests a tactile quality.

Volume  
Volume is the sense of physical mass or thickness an object has. It makes objects feel three‑dimensional and grounded in space.

Contrast  
Contrast is the difference between elements—light vs. dark, warm vs. cool, or large vs. small, that creates emphasis and visual impact.
