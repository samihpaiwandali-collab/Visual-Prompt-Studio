Assignment Title 
Heavy Metal / Gothic 
Medium: Digital Illustration
For my album cover, I want to create a digital illustration of a huge Gothic fortress standing on a rocky cliff during a storm. The fortress will have tall towers, sharp edges, and a dark, mysterious appearance. Near the gate, I will add a small person wearing armour to show the difference in size. I will use deep shadows at the bottom and a bright light shining through the clouds onto the tallest tower.
To create depth, I will place large rocks in the foreground and make the distant towers look lighter and less clear because of the fog. I will also add swirling storm clouds and blowing debris to show movement. My main colours will be black, dark gray, and steel blue to create a cold and dramatic mood. A glowing red symbol on the gate will add contrast. The fortress will cover most of the right side, while the small figure and debris will be on the left, creating an asymmetrical balance.
